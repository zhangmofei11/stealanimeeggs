Heist Guide

Beginner Tips

  • Start with easy levels to learn the layout
  • Watch guard patrol patterns before moving
  • Stick to shadows and avoid lights
  • Carry eggs one at a time initially
  • Build up speed to outrun guards

Advanced Strategies

  • Use decoys to distract guards
  • Time movements with guard blind spots
  • Learn level shortcuts for faster escapes
  • Team up with other players for heists
  • Prioritize high-value eggs over quantity

Level Progression

  • Levels 1-5: Easy intro levels
  • Levels 6-10: More traps and guards
  • Levels 11-15: Stealth becomes harder
  • Levels 16-20: Boss battles with rare eggs
  • Endgame: Maximum security areas

Egg Values

  • Common: 10-50 coins
  • Rare: 100-500 coins
  • Epic: 1000-5000 coins
  • Legendary: 10000+ coins
